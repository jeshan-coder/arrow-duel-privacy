Arrow Duel — Privacy Policy

Published by Bindu Intelligence

Last updated: 16 November 2026

The short version

Arrow Duel is a puzzle game. It has no accounts of its own, no chat and no servers of its own. Your progress lives on your phone and, if you use Google Play Games (optional), also in your Play Games cloud save. We never ask for your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location (the player name in the game is any nickname you like). The only company that receives information from the game is Google: for ads, for game statistics and crash reports (Firebase), for Play Games (sign-in, cloud save, achievements and leaderboards), and for Google Play purchases, updates and reviews.

What stays on your phone

Your progress — the levels you have won, your coins and boosters, your League and World Championship results, your daily rewards and challenges, the name you give yourself and your settings — is saved on your device, using Android's normal app storage. It is never sent to us. The game also makes a random Player ID (for example 4821-7730) so we can find your game if you write to us; it says nothing about who you are.

If Android's own backup is turned on, Android may include your progress in your device backup with Google, so it can come back on a new phone (Android Settings → Google → Backup). Uninstalling the app deletes your progress from your phone (a Play Games cloud save stays, see below).

Google Play Games (optional)

Arrow Duel works with Google Play Games, so you can keep your progress in the cloud, move it to a new phone, unlock achievements and appear on leaderboards. You can play the whole game without it: tap “Not now” when the game asks, or stay signed out.

  • Sign-in. If you have a Play Games profile, Google may sign you in automatically when the game starts. Google then tells the game your Play Games player name and picture, so the game can show “Connected as …”. The game only shows this on your phone and never sends it to us.
  • Cloud save. While you are signed in, the game saves a copy of your progress (the same progress listed above, without your settings) as a Play Games saved game. The game never replaces a cloud save with another game without asking you first; when you choose between two games, the other one is kept as a backup copy.
  • Achievements and leaderboards. The game tells Play Games which achievements you have unlocked and sends three scores: your total stars, your best win streak and your number of World Championship titles. Leaderboards show your Play Games name, picture and score to other players, as your Play Games profile settings allow.

This data is kept by Google with your Play Games profile and is encrypted on its way. We cannot see it. Google also collects diagnostic information about its Play Games software. You can delete Arrow Duel's Play Games data, or your whole Play Games profile, in the Play Games app or your Google Account: Delete your Play Games data.

Your rivals are computer players

Everyone you play against — in the levels, the League and the World Championship — is played by the game itself, on your phone. There is no online play with other people. Apart from the Play Games features above (which you choose), your name and results are never sent anywhere.

What the ads collect

Arrow Duel shows ads from Google AdMob. To do that, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising ID and Android's app set ID,
  • general device and app information (device model, operating system version, app version, language, coarse location derived from your IP address),
  • how you interacted with an ad (app interactions),
  • diagnostic information about how ads load and perform.

Google uses this to choose ads, to measure them and to prevent fraud. Google acts as an independent data controller for this. Their policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

Arrow Duel never sees this data itself.

Game statistics and crash reports (Firebase)

To learn which levels are too hard and to fix problems, the game uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. They may collect:

  • what happens in the game: levels started, won, lost or left (with stars), League, World Championship and Daily Challenge results, how often and how long you play, purchases and ads shown;
  • a random app-instance ID made by Firebase (not your advertising ID: game statistics never use the advertising ID);
  • general device and app information (device model, operating system version, app version, language, country or region from your IP address);
  • if the game crashes: a crash report (where in the game it crashed, device model, operating system and app version, and a random Crashlytics installation ID).

Google processes this for us under the Firebase terms. We see only totals and crash reports, never who you are. Detailed statistics are kept for a limited time (Google Analytics' data-retention setting) and crash reports for 90 days.

Purchases, updates and reviews

In-app purchases (coin packs, the Starter Pack, No Ads and the Champion Pack) are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The game only receives a purchase record (which product, and an order ID) so it can give you what you bought. No Ads and other one-time purchases can be restored on a new phone with Settings → Restore purchases.

The game also uses Google Play to tell you when an update is available and, now and then, to ask whether you would like to rate it. Both are shown and handled by Google Play.

Checking the internet connection

Some modes need an internet connection, and the game plays offline for a few minutes at a time. To know whether you are online, the game asks a tiny Google page (www.gstatic.com/generate_204, the same check Android itself uses). This request contains no information about you; like any web request, it reaches Google with your IP address.

Daily reminders

If you turn on reminders, the game schedules a short daily notification (for example about the Daily Challenge) on your phone. Reminders are made and shown on your device; nothing is sent to us. You can switch them off any time in the game's Settings → Reminders, or in Android's notification settings.

Your choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, a consent form from Google appears before any ad. You choose there whether ads can be personalised and whether data may be used for statistics, and the game follows your choice: without your consent, statistics are sent without any identifier. You can change your mind at any time: Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Everywhere, Android lets you reset or delete your advertising ID: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Ads that give a reward are always optional. Nothing in the game is locked behind watching one.

Children

Arrow Duel is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children, and ad requests are not tagged as child-directed. Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience (rated PG at most). If you believe a child has used the app and you have a concern, contact us at the address below.

Permissions

The app asks for internet and network access (used by Google's ads, Google Play, Play Games and the connection check), notifications and start at boot (to show your daily reminder, and to keep it after the phone restarts), and vibration (game effects, which you can switch off in Settings). The Google libraries also declare the permissions they need to read the advertising ID, to talk to Google Play and to do their own work in the background. Arrow Duel does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location.

If you write to us

When you use Settings → Help & contact, your email app opens with our address and your Player ID in the subject. If you send the email, we receive your email address, your Player ID and what you write. We use them only to answer you, and we delete them if you ask.

Legal basis, how long we keep data, and your rights

Bindu Intelligence is responsible for this app's data (the "controller"). In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, personalised ads and game statistics with identifiers rely on your consent. Non-personalised ads, fraud prevention, crash reports, purchases and the connection check rely on our legitimate interest in running, funding and fixing the game, and on giving you what you bought. Play Games features are used only when you choose them, to give you what they offer.

Your progress stays on your phone until you uninstall the game. A Play Games cloud save, achievements and scores stay with your Play Games profile until you delete them (see “Google Play Games” above). Emails you send us are deleted once your question is answered, or sooner if you ask. Google keeps the data its services collect as its own policies say.

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct or delete it, to limit or object to its use, or to move it; you can withdraw consent at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. Because the game has no accounts of its own, we usually cannot tell which statistics are yours; uninstalling the game starts fresh ones. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

We do not sell your data

We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone other than the Google services described above (ads, Firebase, Play Games and Google Play).
