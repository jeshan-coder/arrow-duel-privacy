Arrow Duel — Privacy Policy

Published by Bindu Intelligence

Last updated: 14 November 2026

The short version

Arrow Duel is a puzzle game. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no servers of its own. Your progress lives on your phone. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. The only company that receives information from the game is Google: for ads and for Google Play purchases, updates and reviews.

What stays on your phone

Your progress — the levels you have won, your coins and boosters, your League and World Championship results, your daily rewards and challenges, the name you give yourself and your settings — is saved on your device, using Android's normal app storage. It is not sent to us. The game also makes a random Player ID (for example 4821-7730) so we can find your game if you write to us; it says nothing about who you are.

If Android's own backup is turned on, Android may include your progress in your device backup with Google, so it can come back on a new phone (Android Settings → Google → Backup). Uninstalling the app deletes your progress from your phone.

Your rivals are computer players

Everyone you play against — in the levels, the League and the World Championship — is played by the game itself, on your phone. There is no online play with other people, and your name and results are never sent anywhere.

What the ads collect

Arrow Duel shows ads from Google AdMob. To do that, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • general device and app information (device model, operating system version, app version, language, coarse location derived from your IP address),
  • how you interacted with an ad.

Google uses this to choose ads, to measure them and to prevent fraud. Google acts as an independent data controller for this. Their policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

Arrow Duel never sees this data itself.

Purchases, updates and reviews

In-app purchases (coin packs, the Starter Pack, No Ads and the Champion Pack) are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The game only receives a purchase record (which product, and an order ID) so it can give you what you bought. No Ads and other one-time purchases can be restored on a new phone with Settings → Restore purchases.

The game also uses Google Play to tell you when an update is available and, now and then, to ask whether you would like to rate it. Both are shown and handled by Google Play.

Checking the internet connection

Some modes need an internet connection, and the game plays offline for a few minutes at a time. To know whether you are online, the game asks a tiny Google page (www.gstatic.com/generate_204, the same check Android itself uses). This request contains no information about you; like any web request, it reaches Google with your IP address.

Daily reminders

If you turn on reminders, the game schedules a short daily notification (for example about the Daily Challenge) on your phone. Reminders are made and shown on your device; nothing is sent to us. You can switch them off any time in the game's Settings → Reminders, or in Android's notification settings.

Your choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, a consent form from Google appears before any ad. You choose there whether ads can be personalised, and the game follows your choice. You can change your mind at any time: Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Everywhere, Android lets you reset or delete your advertising ID: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Ads that give a reward are always optional. Nothing in the game is locked behind watching one.

Children

Arrow Duel is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children, and ad requests are not tagged as child-directed. The maximum ad content rating is set to teen. If you believe a child has used the app and you have a concern, contact us at the address below.

Permissions

The app asks for internet and network access (used by Google's ads, Google Play and the connection check), notifications and start at boot (to show your daily reminder, and to keep it after the phone restarts), and vibration (game effects, which you can switch off in Settings). The Google libraries also declare the permissions they need to read the advertising ID, to talk to Google Play and to do their own work in the background. Arrow Duel does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location.

If you write to us

When you use Settings → Help & contact, your email app opens with our address and your Player ID in the subject. If you send the email, we receive your email address, your Player ID and what you write. We use them only to answer you, and we delete them if you ask.

We do not sell your data

We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone other than the Google services described above.

Changes
